Quill Math Accessibility Initiative

Learn Math Equations

An accessible, screen-reader-first mathematics library dedicated to empowering blind, visually impaired, and sighted learners with 150 foundational equations.

Our Mission & Accessibility Promise

Mathematics is the universal language of science, technology, and engineering, yet standard digital equations often present severe barriers for visually impaired students. Our mission is to make mathematical knowledge universally accessible, intuitive, and navigable for everyone.

♿ Screen Reader First

Full native support for NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, and Orca using clean, un-clipped MathML 3.0 standards to prevent duplicate reading and speech misinterpretations.

🎯 In-Place Navigation

Explore complex fractions, exponents, and nested expressions level by level with single-key keyboard shortcuts without losing your place in the document.

💡 Plain English Intuition

Every single equation includes 3 to 4 lines of clear, concise explanation describing what the formula means, what variables represent, and where it is applied.

⚡ Open Source & Free

Freely available for thousands of students, educators, and assistive technology specialists worldwide.

Interactive Sub-Expression Navigation Guide

When you focus on any equation card in the collections, you can interactively explore its mathematical structure using your keyboard:

Keyboard Key Action Description
Enter / Space / ↓ Open Navigator / Drill Down Enters the equation's internal structure or drills down into a focused sub-term.
→ / ← Next / Previous Term Walks horizontally across sibling terms and operators at the current depth level.
↑ / Esc Go Up Returns to the parent expression enclosing the current term.
Backspace / Q / X Exit Navigation Closes the navigator drawer and returns focus directly to the main equation card.
